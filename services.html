Category 01 • Daily Shift (8–10 Hours) Most Requested

Full-Time Maid Services

Our full-time housemaids provide structured, full-day domestic support (typically 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM or 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM) so your residence stays immaculate without disrupting your family's privacy overnight.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Daily sweeping, mopping, and vacuuming of all rooms
  • Bathroom & washroom sanitation and towel changes
  • Machine & hand laundry, drying, folding, and steam ironing
  • Kitchen counter cleaning, utensil washing, and vegetable prep
  • Dusting furniture, windowsills, and organizing wardrobes
  • Receiving grocery deliveries and maintaining pantry order
Service Suitability

Ideal for 2 to 5 BHK family residences, dual-income couples, and households with children or pets requiring consistent all-day upkeep.

Key Benefits

Single reliable helper for all daily chores, fixed arrival/departure discipline, and no live-in accommodation required.

Category 02 • Dedicated Slot (2–5 Hours)

Part-Time Maid Services

Designed for apartments and busy professionals who need dependable daily assistance during fixed morning or evening hours without committing to a full-day shift.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Daily floor sweeping, wet mopping, and surface dusting
  • Dishwashing and kitchen slab/sink sanitation
  • Loading laundry machine, drying clothes, and basic folding
  • Scheduled bathroom cleaning and trash disposal
Service Suitability

Best suited for 1 to 3 BHK apartments, single professionals, and young couples who spend most of the day at work.

Key Benefits

Cost-effective monthly structure, neighborhood-proximate candidates for high punctuality, and verified local address records.

Category 03 • 24-Hour Residential Support

Live-In Domestic Help

Our live-in domestic helpers reside within your household premises, providing round-the-clock dependability for families with early mornings, late work schedules, young children, or senior family members.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Comprehensive morning-to-evening household cleaning
  • Early morning tea/breakfast prep & evening kitchen support
  • Complete family laundry, ironing, and bed-making
  • Assisting with children's routines or senior family members
  • Accompanying family during travel or domestic relocations
  • Maintaining home security awareness when family is away
Service Suitability

Ideal for large family homes, villas, working parents with infants, and households with dedicated staff quarters or private sleeping space.

Key Benefits

Zero daily commute delays, permanent address & family guardian verification, and clearly defined rest hours & leave calendars.

Category 04 • Hospitality-Grade Residence Upkeep

Professional Housekeeping

For fine residences, penthouses, and estates with delicate materials, our hospitality-trained executive housekeepers bring hotel-standard attention to surface conservation, linen care, and formal etiquette.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Specialized care for Italian marble, hardwood, and brassware
  • Delicate couture wardrobe care, steaming, and shoe care
  • Formal table setting, guest reception, and refreshment service
  • Managing household consumables & supervising maintenance staff
Service Suitability

Tailored for luxury apartments, duplexes, diplomatic residences, and families who frequently host guests.

Key Benefits

Candidates tested on chemical-safe surface care, discreet professional demeanor, and prior luxury estate references.

Category 05 • Culinary & Dietary Specialists

Home Cooks

Enjoy wholesome, freshly prepared meals every day without kitchen stress. Our home cooks are evaluated for personal hygiene, culinary versatility, spice control, and organized kitchen management.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Preparing fresh breakfast, lunch, school tiffins, and dinner
  • Regional Indian (North, South, Gujarati, Bengali, Maharashtrian)
  • Continental, salads, soups, and high-protein or low-oil diets
  • Sanitizing produce, organizing fridge storage, and grocery lists
  • Maintaining clean stovetops, appliances, and prep counters
  • Adapting meals for seniors, toddlers, or Jain/vegan rules
Service Suitability

Available as twice-daily part-time cooks (morning & evening slots), full-time kitchen managers, or live-in family cooks.

Key Benefits

Live cooking trial before final confirmation, strict personal hygiene standards, and zero reliance on outside takeout.

Category 06 • Infant, Toddler & After-School Care

Babysitters and Nannies

Childcare placements demand our highest level of reference scrutiny and temperament evaluation. We place patient, affectionate nannies and babysitters who prioritize child safety, hygiene, and developmental routines.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Infant feeding, burping, bottle sterilization & weaning prep
  • Oil massage, warm bathing, diapering, and sleep routines
  • Engaging toddlers in storybooks, puzzles, and park visits
  • Sanitizing toys, washing baby garments, and nursery upkeep
  • School bus pick-up/drop-off and after-school snack routines
  • Maintaining daily feeding & activity logs for working parents
Service Suitability

Working parents returning from parental leave, families with twins, and households seeking dedicated 10-hour day or 24-hour live-in nannies.

Key Benefits

Mandatory verification of at least 2 prior childcare employer references, hygiene screening, and supervised parent-present trial days.

Category 07 • Dignified Senior Care & Companionship

Elderly Care Assistance

Supporting aging parents at home requires patience, physical attentiveness, and emotional warmth. Our male and female senior care attendants provide reliable non-medical daily assistance and reassuring companionship.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Walking support, fall prevention, and wheelchair transfers
  • Timely medication reminders, hydration, and meal assistance
  • Dignified assistance with bathing, grooming, and dressing
  • Assisting with prescribed home physiotherapy exercises
  • Logging basic daily vitals (BP, blood sugar, temperature)
  • Accompanying seniors to clinic visits, parks, and social outings
Service Suitability

Available in 12-hour day shifts, 12-hour night shifts, or 24-hour live-in arrangements for seniors living with family or independently.

Key Benefits

Screened for calm temperament, emergency escalation awareness, and verified prior senior-care family references.
