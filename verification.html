Step-by-Step Screening Architecture

The 8-Stage Candidate Verification Journey

Each stage is logged in the candidate's file and summarized in the Pre-Placement Verification Dossier shared with your family.

01
Stage 01 • Identity

Identity Document Verification

Candidates must present original government-issued photo identification (such as Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, or Driving License). Our verification desk cross-checks name spelling, date of birth, legal adult working age (18+ years), and photograph match against original records.

Original ID Inspected Age Eligibility (18+) Recent Photograph Logged
02
Stage 02 • Residence

Current & Permanent Address Verification

We document both the candidate's current local residential address and their permanent hometown address using utility bills, rental agreements, ration cards, or family contact verification so residential traceability is established.

Local Address Proof Permanent Address Logged Next-of-Kin Contact
03
Stage 03 • Community & Family

Personal & Family Reference Checks

Particularly critical for live-in placements, our counselors speak directly with two independent personal or family references to verify the candidate's family background, stability, and family awareness/support of their work schedule.

2 Personal References Live-In Guardian Consent
04
Stage 04 • Work Track Record

Previous Employment Checks

Our placement coordinators conduct structured phone interviews with previous household employers. We verify dates of employment, primary duties handled, punctuality, honesty, behavior around children/seniors, and the reason for leaving.

Tenure Confirmed Conduct & Punctuality Reason for Leaving
05
Stage 05 • Legal & Police Check

Background Checks (Where Legally Available & Authorized)

With the candidate's explicit written consent, we conduct searches across publicly accessible digital court records and assist families in submitting official local police domestic-help verification forms in accordance with municipal guidelines.

Signed Candidate Consent Public Court Record Check Police Form Facilitation
06
Stage 06 • Practical Competence

Skills & Experience Assessment

Candidates are evaluated on role-specific competencies: appliance safety and fabric care for housemaids; kitchen hygiene and meal preparation for cooks; sterilization and emergency awareness for nannies; and safe mobility transfer for elderly caregivers.

Hygiene & Safety Protocol Appliance Operation Role Skill Grading
07
Stage 07 • Temperament & Fit

Interview & Suitability Assessment

A senior placement counselor conducts a 1-on-1 interview to evaluate communication clarity, personal grooming, emotional maturity, comfort with household pets, dietary preferences, and long-term commitment.

Counselor Interview Expectation Alignment
08
Stage 08 • Onboarding

Final Placement & Supervised Trial Process

We arrange family interviews, coordinate a 2- to 3-day supervised paid trial in your home, execute a written placement agreement detailing duties, salary, and weekly offs, and schedule 30/90/180-day counselor check-ins.

2–3 Day Home Trial Written Agreement Post-Placement Check-Ins

Our Commitment to Honest, Lawful & Realistic Screening

At Hearth & Haven, transparency means being forthright about both the rigor of our screening process and the legal boundaries that govern private staffing agencies.

1. Dependence on Applicable Laws, Consent & Official Records

All identity checks, address verifications, reference inquiries, and background checks are performed strictly within the framework of applicable data privacy and labor laws. Criminal database checks and local police verification procedures require the explicit written consent of the candidate and depend on the availability, accuracy, and processing timelines of municipal police departments and public court registries.

2. Why We Never Claim "100% Risk-Free" or "Guaranteed Safety"

While multi-stage verification verifies historical records and prior employer feedback, human behavior in a household setting can never be predicted with absolute certainty. Any agency claiming "100% guaranteed safety" or "zero-risk placements" is making an unsupported claim. Instead, we combine thorough pre-placement screening with practical household precautions.

Recommended Household Safety Practices

We advise all client families to pair our agency screening with sensible household practices:

  • Always complete the local police station domestic-helper registration form in your jurisdiction (our counselors prepare the paperwork).
  • Be present during the initial 2- to 3-day trial period to supervise routines and set clear household boundaries.
  • Keep cash, jewelry, and sensitive financial documents secured in locked storage.
  • Use common-area cameras transparently (never in private bathrooms or staff sleeping quarters) with prior disclosure.
