Our Screening Architecture

How We Screen & Verify Every Candidate

Before any candidate is introduced to your family, they complete our structured screening workflow. You receive a clear verification summary dossier prior to conducting your interview.

Full 8-Stage Verification Guide
Stage 01–02

Identity & Address Verification

Cross-verification of official government photo ID, age eligibility, current local residence, and permanent home address records.

Stage 03–04

Reference & Employment History

Structured phone verification with previous household employers and family references to confirm tenure, conduct, and reliability.

Stage 05

Authorized Background Checks

Public court database checks and police verification facilitation conducted strictly where legally available and with written candidate consent.

Stage 06–08

Skill & Suitability Evaluation

Practical evaluation of culinary, childcare, or housekeeping competence alongside an in-person counselor interview.

Transparency & Compliance Standard: Verification procedures are performed in accordance with applicable laws, written candidate consent, and the availability of official or public records. While thorough screening significantly reduces placement risk, no ethical agency can claim 100% risk-free placements; we always advise families to participate in trial days and follow prudent home security practices.

Simple 4-Step Placement Journey

How Our Placement Process Works

From your initial consultation to post-placement follow-ups, a dedicated placement counselor guides you through every step.

01 Day 1

Share Household Needs

Tell us your required role, work hours, household size, dietary preferences, and specific expectations via our form or counselor call.

02 Days 2–3

Review Verified Dossiers

Receive 2 to 4 curated candidate profiles complete with verification records, work history, salary expectations, and counselor notes.

03 Days 4–6

Interview & Supervised Trial

Conduct video or in-person interviews and schedule a paid 2- to 3-day trial period in your home to evaluate practical compatibility.

04 Ongoing

Placement & Support

Sign a transparent placement agreement with clear duty schedules, attendance standards, and 6-month replacement support.
