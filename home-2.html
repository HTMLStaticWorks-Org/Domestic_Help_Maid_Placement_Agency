The Verification Difference

Why Structured Agency Screening Matters

Hiring through unverified word-of-mouth or informal brokers often exposes families to undocumented identities, sudden absenteeism, and zero recourse. Compare our disciplined protocol below.

Realistic & Lawful Screening

All background and police verification checks are conducted strictly where legally permitted and with the candidate's signed authorization. We never make exaggerated claims of "100% risk-free guarantees."
