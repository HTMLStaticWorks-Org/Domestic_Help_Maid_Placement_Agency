Client Terms & Ethical Placement Charter

1. Confidential Candidate Dossiers

Candidate verification dossiers shared with registered client families contain sensitive personal information and may be used solely for evaluating household employment with Hearth & Haven.

2. Ethical Working Conditions & Direct Wages

Client families agree to provide safe working conditions, agreed rest periods, weekly leaves, and direct monthly salary payments to placed domestic staff without arbitrary deductions.

3. Verification & Legal Scope

All screening checks are performed in accordance with applicable laws, written candidate consent, and official record availability. Families are encouraged to complete local municipal police verification and participate in the supervised trial period.
