About Hearth & Haven

Bringing Structure, Dignity & Trust to Domestic Staffing.

Founded in 2016, Hearth & Haven was created to bridge a critical gap in urban households: replacing informal, undocumented domestic hiring with a transparent, counselor-guided placement agency that respects both family safety and domestic worker dignity.

Consult Our Placement Team Our Screening Protocol
Our Mission

Dependable Household Support Without Guesswork

To empower families, working parents, and senior citizens with thoroughly vetted domestic staff through documented identity checks, verified employer references, and clear written placement agreements.

Our Vision

Professionalizing the Domestic Care Ecosystem

To set the national benchmark for ethical household staffing—where families enjoy long-term peace of mind and domestic workers receive fair compensation, safe working environments, and respected career progression.

Our Commitment

Accountability Beyond Day One

Our work does not end when a helper starts. Through supervised trial days, 30/90/180-day counselor check-ins, and a 6-month replacement policy, we stay accountable throughout the placement lifecycle.

Professional Standards & Screening Philosophy

Dual-Sided Responsibility: Protecting Families & Respecting Staff

Long-lasting domestic placements happen only when both the employing family and the placed helper experience mutual respect and clarity. Our Code of Practice enforces strict standards on both sides of every placement.

Our Approach to Candidate Screening

We reject mass database scraping. Every candidate is interviewed in person by our placement counselors, assessed for practical hygiene and role skills, and cleared through government ID, dual-address, and prior employer reference verification before their profile is ever shared.

Ethical Worker Welfare Charter

We strictly verify adult working age (18+ years), prohibit any salary withholding or intermediary wage cuts, require humane working hours and private sleeping space for live-in staff, and ensure timely monthly direct salary payments from families to workers.

Why Families Choose Hearth & Haven

6 Pillars That Differentiate Our Agency

1. Written Pre-Interview Verification Dossiers

Review documented ID, address, and employer call notes before conducting your interview.

2. Dedicated Single Point-of-Contact Counselor

One senior counselor manages your brief, interviews, trial feedback, and post-placement support.

3. Zero Hidden Fees & Direct Worker Salaries

One transparent agency placement fee; 100% of monthly wages go directly to your domestic helper.

4. Mandatory 2- to 3-Day Paid Home Trial

Test real-world kitchen, childcare, or housekeeping compatibility before finalizing.

5. Police Verification Paperwork Assistance

We prepare and facilitate local jurisdiction police registration forms with candidate consent.
